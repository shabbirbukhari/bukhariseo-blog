Internal Linking Strategy for Growth

Internal links help customers and Google find important pages — natural and helpful.

Bukhari SEO Editorial Team · Originally published September 13, 2026 · Reviewed November 21, 2026

Internal links help customers and Google find important pages — natural and helpful.

Search visibility is only useful when it helps the right people understand your business and take an informed next step. Use the guidance below as a practical starting point, then adapt it to your services, customers and actual operating area.

What to focus on

Link from relevant guides to the service or product page that answers the next question. Use descriptive anchor text that tells readers what they will find. Link between related articles where the connection is genuinely useful, and make sure important pages are not buried several clicks deep.

Simple steps you can apply

  • Link from homepage to most important service pages
  • Within each post, link to one related deeper guide
  • Use descriptive anchor text — not just click here
  • Link to contact page when you mention getting help
  • Add breadcrumb for large sites with many services
  • Fix broken internal links found in Search Console
  • Keep number of links natural — helpful, not stuffed

Create links that help people continue

Review links whenever a page is updated or a URL changes. Replace broken links and avoid repeating exact-match keyword anchors everywhere; natural, varied wording is clearer for readers.

Common mistake to avoid

Adding no internal links at all — Google and customers struggle to find important pages.

How to review your progress

Write down the page or business profile you changed, the date, and the reason for the change. Check whether the page is accessible, whether customers can use it comfortably on a phone, and whether enquiries or other meaningful actions improve over time. Use Search Console for search performance and Analytics for activity on the website. Avoid judging success from one keyword or a short-term ranking movement.
