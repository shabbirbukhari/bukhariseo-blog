About Bukhari SEO

Bukhari SEO publishes practical search engine optimisation guidance for businesses, shops and service providers. This blog focuses on explaining website and local search concepts in clear language, with examples relevant to Lahore where appropriate.

What this blog aims to provide

Our aim is to help readers understand SEO fundamentals, assess their websites and make more informed decisions. Articles are educational and should be adapted to each business's circumstances.

Editorial approach

We aim to explain recommendations clearly, distinguish general guidance from platform requirements, and link to official documentation when it is useful. Search engines and their policies change, so readers should check current official guidance before making important technical changes.
