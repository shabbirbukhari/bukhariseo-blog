How to Get More Customer Reviews

Reviews help Maps ranking and trust. How to ask right after service via WhatsApp and QR code.

Bukhari SEO Editorial Team · Originally published September 09, 2026 · Reviewed November 21, 2026

Reviews help Maps ranking and trust. How to ask right after service via WhatsApp and QR code.

Search visibility is only useful when it helps the right people understand your business and take an informed next step. Use the guidance below as a practical starting point, then adapt it to your services, customers and actual operating area.

What to focus on

Invite all customers to share an honest review after a genuine interaction. Make the process easy with a direct review link or QR code, and respond calmly to both positive and negative feedback. A useful response addresses the concern without exposing private customer information.

Simple steps you can apply

  • Ask for review right after service is completed and customer is happy
  • Send direct review link via WhatsApp with short thank you note
  • Place QR code for reviews at reception and on invoice
  • Train staff to mention reviews politely at end of service
  • Thank every reviewer by name and mention service received
  • Share best reviews on site with permission and link to profile
  • Keep reviews steady — few each week better than many at once

Ask for honest feedback without pressure

Do not offer discounts, gifts or other incentives for reviews, and do not ask only satisfied customers to post publicly. Never write reviews for customers or use fabricated accounts.

Common mistake to avoid

Offering incentive for reviews or asking only when you know it will be positive — against policy and loses trust.

How to review your progress

Write down the page or business profile you changed, the date, and the reason for the change. Check whether the page is accessible, whether customers can use it comfortably on a phone, and whether enquiries or other meaningful actions improve over time. Use Search Console for search performance and Analytics for activity on the website. Avoid judging success from one keyword or a short-term ranking movement.
