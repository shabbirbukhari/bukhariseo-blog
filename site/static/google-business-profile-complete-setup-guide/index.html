Google Business Profile: Complete Setup Guide

Complete guide to set up your Business Profile — category, hours, services, photos, Q&A, reviews.

Bukhari SEO Editorial Team · Originally published September 07, 2026 · Reviewed November 21, 2026

Complete guide to set up your Business Profile — category, hours, services, photos, Q&A, reviews.

Search visibility is only useful when it helps the right people understand your business and take an informed next step. Use the guidance below as a practical starting point, then adapt it to your services, customers and actual operating area.

What to focus on

Keep the business name, address or service area, phone, website, hours and categories accurate. Add real photographs and update special hours when needed. Choose categories that describe the actual business, not every service it hopes to rank for.

Simple steps you can apply

  • Choose primary category that matches main service exactly
  • Add secondary categories only if you truly offer them
  • Fill hours, including special hours for holidays
  • Add all services with brief, helpful descriptions
  • Upload logo, cover, and recent work photos weekly
  • Use Products or Services section to list popular packages
  • Enable messaging and reply quickly via Business Profile app

Maintain the profile after verification

Do not create duplicate profiles for the same location or use a virtual office as though it were a staffed customer-facing premises. Check Google's eligibility and representation rules before changing the profile.

Common mistake to avoid

Leaving Business Profile half empty with no photos, no services, and no description — lowers Maps visibility.

How to review your progress

Write down the page or business profile you changed, the date, and the reason for the change. Check whether the page is accessible, whether customers can use it comfortably on a phone, and whether enquiries or other meaningful actions improve over time. Use Search Console for search performance and Analytics for activity on the website. Avoid judging success from one keyword or a short-term ranking movement.
