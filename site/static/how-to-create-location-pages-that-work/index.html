How to Create Location Pages That Work

Location pages work when truly unique — write about area, add review from area, map embed.

Bukhari SEO Editorial Team · Originally published September 17, 2026 · Reviewed November 21, 2026

Location pages work when truly unique — write about area, add review from area, map embed.

Search visibility is only useful when it helps the right people understand your business and take an informed next step. Use the guidance below as a practical starting point, then adapt it to your services, customers and actual operating area.

What to focus on

A useful location page explains the service available in that area, practical coverage, travel or delivery details, relevant examples and how to contact the business. Use accurate local information and avoid implying that a branch exists where there is none.

Simple steps you can apply

  • Write one page per area you truly serve with team on ground
  • Mention specific landmark, road, or block you serve in that area
  • Add a customer story or review from that specific area
  • Include map embed and directions from main road
  • Add unique service detail relevant to that area — e.g., same-day in DHA
  • Link to main service page and back to area hub
  • Keep contact and hours accurate for each area

Make each location page genuinely distinct

If all location pages contain the same text with only the neighbourhood swapped, consolidate them or add genuinely useful detail before publishing. A smaller set of helpful pages is better than a large set of doorway pages.

Common mistake to avoid

Creating location pages for areas you do not serve just to capture keywords — leads to poor experience.

How to review your progress

Write down the page or business profile you changed, the date, and the reason for the change. Check whether the page is accessible, whether customers can use it comfortably on a phone, and whether enquiries or other meaningful actions improve over time. Use Search Console for search performance and Analytics for activity on the website. Avoid judging success from one keyword or a short-term ranking movement.
